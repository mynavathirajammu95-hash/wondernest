What it does

A patient helper for every question

WonderNest is built to help students understand, so they can answer the next question on their own.

Ask anything, any time

Students can ask about homework, a confusing lesson or something they're curious about, and get a clear, friendly answer.

Step-by-step explanations

Big ideas are broken into small steps, with hints first, so students work things out instead of copying an answer.

Practice that adapts

Quick quizzes and practice questions get easier or harder based on how a student is doing.

Follows the school's lessons

Help stays connected to what students are learning in class, so it supports their teachers.

A safe space to learn

Access is limited to students of partner schools, so WonderNest stays a focused place for learning.

Encouraging, never judging

Students can ask the same thing twice, or ten times. WonderNest keeps explaining in new ways until it clicks.

How it works

From school sign-up to first question

WonderNest reaches students through their school, so every student who uses it belongs to a partner school.

The school partners with us

A school leader gets in touch, and we set WonderNest up for their classes.

Students get their login

The school shares sign-in details with each student. No public sign-ups.

Learning starts

Students download the app, sign in and start asking questions, in class or at home.

FAQ

Common questions

Who can use WonderNest?

WonderNest is only available to students of our partner schools. Students sign in with details provided by their school.

My child's school isn't a partner yet. Can we still sign up?

Not individually. Ask your school to get in touch with us using the partner form, and we'll take it from there.

Does WonderNest just give students the answers?

No. WonderNest explains ideas step by step and offers hints first, so students build understanding and can solve the next problem themselves.

I forgot my login. What should I do?

Your sign-in details come from your school, so your teacher or school office can help you get back in.

Which devices does it work on?

WonderNest is a mobile app for phones and tablets. Download it from the App Store or Google Play.
